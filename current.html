Some background for curious people

Who am I?

Hi, I’m Borey, a student from Cambodia with a deep interest in mathematics and programming. I am finishing high school and plan to study computer science and mathematics. Curiosity is usually where my work begins: I like taking an idea apart, understanding how it works, and turning it into something useful.

What do I study?

Mathematics has been a long-running passion of mine. I have studied through Cambodia Math Camp at the Royal University of Phnom Penh, MathWOOT from Art of Problem Solving, and the Olympiad Training for Individual Study program. I also took part in the International Mathematical Olympiad in 2025 and 2026, and I continue to prepare for the next challenge.

What do I make?

I began learning web development in grade seven and have continued teaching myself ever since. Today I build websites, small tools, and technical experiments that help me connect abstract ideas with practical results. I am using this time to make new projects, strengthen my skills, and learn across different fields.

Why this page?

This is a simple home for a short introduction to who I am and what I care about. It will grow and change as I do. If you would like to talk about mathematics, code, or a project, write to me at son.norint@gmail.com.
