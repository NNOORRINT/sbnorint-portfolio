Wrist band

A wearable safety device that detects nearby vehicle and alarm sounds, then converts alerts into vibration.

Field

Event

Overview

The wrist band analyzes incoming sound waves to identify possible vehicles and alarms.

When a warning sound is detected, vibration gives people with hearing difficulties an immediate physical alert that can help them stay aware and avoid danger.
